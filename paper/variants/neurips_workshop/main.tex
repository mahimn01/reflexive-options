\begin{abstract}
We construct a reduced-form physical-measure model in which gamma-shaped book
pressure interacts with a price--memory--variance loop. Its equilibrium remains
fixed as coupling varies and its variance drift points inward at zero. For a
centered Gaussian density of signed dealer positions, the local Hopf equation is
quadratic in coupling. An illustrative, non-calibrated configuration gives
$\kappa^\star=31.4933$ yr$^{-1}$, $\omega^\star=47.1186$ rad yr$^{-1}$,
and $\ell_1=-6.2888$; the actual nonlinear book produces a nearby attracting
cycle with positive variance. The kernel shapes a postulated pressure term
rather than deriving hedge flow. Public open interest does not identify dealer
sign, so the pre-extraction WRDS protocol is participant-sign-free and
predictive. No registered market dataset is analyzed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model and measurement boundary}

Option hedging can create state-dependent underlying demand
\citep{freystremme1997,sircarpapanicolaou1998}. Demand-based option pricing
\citep{garleanu2009demand} does not itself derive a spot drift, so our feedback
is a reduced-form physical-measure hypothesis. Let
$X_t=\log(S_t/F_t)$ for a predictable reference and specify
\begin{align}
dX_t&=[-\delta X_t-\tfrac12(v_t-\theta_v)+\kappa g(X_t,v_t,\chi_t)]dt
+\sqrt{v_t}\,dW_t^S,\label{eq:x}\\
dv_t&=[\kappa_v(\theta_v-v_t)+\gamma v_t\chi_t]dt
+\xi\sqrt{v_t}\,dW_t^v,\label{eq:v}\\
d\chi_t&=\alpha(\beta X_t-\chi_t)dt.\label{eq:chi}
\end{align}
At $v=0$, drift is $\kappa_v\theta_v>0$ and diffusion is zero. Thus the added
feedback is boundary-compatible, although we do not prove global non-explosion.

A Gaussian mass in fixed log moneyness integrates against Black--Scholes gamma
as
\begin{align}
\mathcal G(X,v)&=\{2\pi\tau^2(v)\}^{-1/2}e^{-q_dT-X}
\exp[-(X-m(v))^2/(2\tau^2(v))],\nonumber\\
\tau^2(v)&=\sigma_q^2+vT,\qquad
m(v)=\mu_q-(r-q_d+v/2)T.\label{eq:book}
\end{align}
We center and normalize it:
\begin{equation}
g(X,v,\chi)=s\frac{\mathcal G(X,v)-\mathcal G(0,\theta_v)}
{\mathcal G(0,\theta_v)},\qquad s\in\{-1,+1\}.\label{eq:center}
\end{equation}
Then $(0,\theta_v,0)$ is an equilibrium for every $\kappa$. The orientation
$s$ describes latent signed dealer positions. Public OI counts contracts but
does not identify participant or side \citep{oicopeninterest}; proprietary
participant-tagged data are used when actual market-maker positions are needed
\citep{vasquez2025zerodte}.

\section{Local bifurcation}

At the fixed equilibrium, put $G_X=g_X$, $G_v=g_v$, $G_\chi=g_\chi$. The
Jacobian is
\begin{equation}
J=\begin{pmatrix}a&b&d\\0&-\kappa_v&c\\m&0&-\alpha\end{pmatrix},
\quad
\begin{matrix}a=-\delta+\kappa G_X,&b=-1/2+\kappa G_v,\\
d=\kappa G_\chi,&c=\gamma\theta_v,&m=\alpha\beta.
\end{matrix}\label{eq:J}
\end{equation}
For $\det(\lambda I-J)=\lambda^3+c_2\lambda^2+c_1\lambda+c_0$,
\begin{align*}
c_2&=\kappa_v+\alpha-a,\quad
c_1=\kappa_v\alpha-a(\kappa_v+\alpha)-dm,\\
c_0&=-a\kappa_v\alpha-bcm-dm\kappa_v.
\end{align*}

\begin{theorem}
If the drift is $C^3$ and, at $\kappa^\star>0$,
$c_2,c_1,c_0>0$, $H:=c_1c_2-c_0=0$, $H'\ne0$, and
$\ell_1\ne0$, then the fixed equilibrium undergoes a non-degenerate local
Hopf bifurcation. Its eigenvalues are $\pm i\sqrt{c_1}$ and $-c_2$, with
\[
\left.\frac{d\operatorname{Re}\lambda_+}{d\kappa}\right|_{\kappa^\star}
=-\frac{H'(\kappa^\star)}{2(c_1+c_2^2)}.
\]
In the \citet{kuznetsov2004} convention, $\ell_1<0$ gives stable small cycles
on the side where the equilibrium pair is unstable.
\end{theorem}

For \eqref{eq:center}, $G_\chi=0$. With
$A=\alpha+\kappa_v$, $M=\alpha\kappa_v$, and
$L=(\gamma\theta_v)(\alpha\beta)$,
\begin{equation}
H(\kappa)=G_X^2A\kappa^2+\{G_vL-G_XA(A+2\delta)\}\kappa
+A(M+\delta A+\delta^2)-L/2.\label{eq:H}
\end{equation}
Every root still requires the three positivity conditions and transversality.
Negative discriminant means only no local Hopf root. Analytic derivatives of
\eqref{eq:book} through order three enter the standard first-Lyapunov formula;
the variance tensor includes $B_{v,v\chi}=B_{v,\chi v}=\gamma$.

\section{Actual-nonlinearity validation}

For the illustrative, non-fitted configuration
$(\delta,\kappa_v,\theta_v,\alpha,\beta,\gamma)
=(0.5,40,0.0625,50,2,500)$ and
$(\mu_q,\sigma_q,T,s)=(0.06,0.20,0.25,+1)$,
$G_X=-0.0617978$ and $G_v=-2.0198878$. Enumerating both quadratic roots gives
\[
\kappa^\star=31.4932976,\qquad \omega^\star=47.1185670,\qquad
\ell_1=-6.2888041,
\]
with crossing speed $0.2686294$. The period is 0.13335 years.
The remote second valid root is $\kappa^{\star\star}=16860.8961$, where the
pair crosses back into the stable half-plane; threshold plots use the first root.

Direct integration of \eqref{eq:x}--\eqref{eq:chi} with the actual Gaussian
book gives $X\in[-0.02814,0.02358]$ and
$v\in[0.04453,0.08187]$ after transient discard. The displayed cycle therefore
stays in the local, positive-variance region. This validates an internally
consistent possibility, not SPX calibration or global stability.
Gross-normalized signed mixtures in the full paper show that supercriticality
is book-dependent; dispersed or offsetting books can instead be subcritical.

\section{Pre-extraction protocol and limitations}

Before expected September 2026 WRDS access, Amendments A13--A16 fix the
registered historical horizon from 2017-01-03 through 2024-10-29. Four observable
OI-and-pricing-derived summaries---log nonnegative mass, call--put composition, mean log
moneyness, and log dispersion---separately predict
$\log(r_{t+1}^2+10^{-8})$ with leads and HAR-style lags constructed on the
complete CRSP market calendar, official Cboe VIX, log spot, a linear trend,
outcome-session weekday indicators, and one monthly-expiration control.
Contracts use fractional settlement time, fixed liquidity/IV/moneyness filters,
and parity forwards split by AM/PM settlement; OI is availability-timed.
HAC lag 22 and a 22-session complete-calendar bootstrap are separately BH-adjusted over
four tests. Robust association requires rejection by both adjusted families
and a 95\% bootstrap interval excluding zero; one-family rejection is
method-sensitive. Convention-signed GEX and stress interactions are secondary.
This is a retrospective pre-analysis plan over historically public outcomes,
not a blinded prospective experiment.

The theorem is local, deterministic, and specific to the detrended-log-price
coordinate. Brownian paths are not periodic orbits, and no global attractor or
stochastic threshold is proved. The pressure term is reduced-form, while the
price--memory--variance channel is independently assumed. Any WRDS association
concerns public book summaries; confirming signed dealer feedback requires
participant-tagged positions and an identification strategy.
