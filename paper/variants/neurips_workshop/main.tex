\begin{abstract}
We introduce a 3D reflexive stochastic-volatility SDE $(S_t, v_t, \chi_t)$
coupling the spot to its own option market via a
G\^arleanu--Pedersen--Poteshman dealer-gamma drift, prove a Hopf
bifurcation theorem with closed-form first Lyapunov coefficient $\ell_1$
and threshold $\kappa^\star$ for log-normal open-interest, and pre-register
an evaluation framework whose primary test is a direct, model-free
dealer-gamma (GEX) regression---with a sliced-$W_2$ surface metric and a
critical-slowing-down detector---anchored to a git commit hash with an
OpenTimestamps Bitcoin proof. We additionally give a Hawkes--SV
criticality correspondence: \citet{hardiman2013}'s empirical $n\approx 1$
sits at the real-eigenvalue (saddle-node) stratum, while the model's Hopf
is the strictly stronger oscillatory cell beyond any scalar branching
ratio.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and contributions}
\label{sec:intro}

Real options markets exhibit endogenous volatility cycles that pure
stochastic-volatility (SV) models cannot reproduce. The empirical anchor is
\citet{hardiman2013}, whose Hawkes branching ratio for E-mini mid-price
changes sits at $n \approx 1$ throughout 1998--2011---the critical edge of
endogenous-event criticality. With generative-model and RL-based market
emulators \citep{vuleticcont2025volgan,buehler2019deep,hesuttergonon2025}
now standard infrastructure for derivatives pricing, hedging and risk,
we address: (i) what continuous-time mechanism produces this critical
reflexivity, and (ii) how should an evaluation pipeline for
reflexivity-aware generative models be locked \emph{before} empirical
contact, in a manner that addresses the documented computational-
reproducibility crisis in finance \citep{perignon2024repro}?

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(C1) a 3D reflexive SV SDE with explicit dealer-gamma feedback from the OI
grid via \citet{garleanu2009demand};
(C2) a Hopf bifurcation theorem on the deterministic skeleton with
\emph{closed-form} $\ell_1$ and $\kappa^\star$ for log-normal OI in
moneyness;
(C3) a numerical $(\xi,\rho,\sigma_v)$ phase diagram locating $\kappa^\star$
in the $0.85$--$0.95$ band across the SPX-relevant corner (whether the
market sits near $\kappa^\star$ is deferred to the empirical GEX test);
(C4) a \emph{pre-registered} evaluation framework whose primary test is a
direct dealer-gamma (GEX) regression, with a critical-slowing-down
detector, block-bootstrap CIs, BH--FDR correction, and TOST equivalence; and
(C5) synthetic-pipeline validation: the locked protocol returns the
predicted SW2 ordering on data with known underlying physics.

\paragraph{Why this matters for GenAI in finance.} As generative market
models proliferate \citep{choudhary2024funvol,issa2023signature,
jin2025diffusion,cohenreisingerwang}, evaluators that discriminate
\emph{realism} from \emph{regime-shift sensitivity} on the \emph{same}
agent across an environmental sweep---with pre-committed multiple-testing
corrections---are scarce. The closed-form $\kappa^\star$ and $\ell_1$
pin down where dealer-gamma feedback flips a stable Heston regime into
an attracting limit cycle. \citet{halperin2025marketron} is the
nearest such model but uses a latent stochastic memory and no bifurcation analysis;
\citet{dai2025gamma} share the dealer-gamma axis but produce only a
saddle-node onset; \citet{hesuttergonon2025} share the
RL-with-environmental-sweep idea but train separate agents per grid
point. Full preprint with proofs and appendix A on arXiv [anonymous].

\section{The reflexive model}
\label{sec:model}

Let $S_t$ be the spot, $v_t$ the variance, and $\chi_t$ a memory variable. The
reflexive system is
\begin{align}
\frac{dS_t}{S_t} &= \bigl(\mu + \kappa\, G(S_t, \chi_t, v_t)\bigr)\, dt + \sigma(S_t, v_t)\, dW^S_t, \\
dv_t &= \bigl(\kappa_v(\theta_v - v_t) + \gamma\, \chi_t\bigr)\, dt + \xi \sqrt{v_t}\, dW^v_t, \\
d\chi_t &= \bigl(-\alpha\, \chi_t + \beta\, \log(S_t / S_0)\bigr)\, dt,
\end{align}
with $d\langle W^S, W^v\rangle = \rho\, dt$, reflexive coupling
$\kappa \geq 0$, leverage strength $\gamma \geq 0$, memory decay
$\alpha > 0$, and $G$ the aggregate dealer gamma computed from the OI grid
(coupling motivated by GPP demand pressure). At $\kappa = \gamma = 0$ the system
collapses to time-dependent Heston \citep{heston1993} (verified in
\texttt{tests/test\_simulator.py}).

\paragraph{Why three states.} A 2D reduction (drop $\chi_t$ and $\gamma \chi$)
has Jacobian
$J_{2D} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} a(\kappa) & b(\kappa) \\ 0 & -\kappa_v\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$,
upper triangular with real eigenvalues $\{a(\kappa), -\kappa_v\}$: only
saddle-node bifurcations are admissible. To Hopf, the feedback must flow
in both directions, which the leverage channel $\gamma \chi$ together with
the auxiliary memory equation supplies. This is the structural reason
\citet{dai2025gamma} get a saddle-node onset where we get a Hopf.

\section{Hopf threshold and closed-form first Lyapunov coefficient}
\label{sec:hopf}

Linearise the deterministic skeleton at the equilibrium
$(S^\star, \theta_v, \chi^\star)$, in deviation variables $(y, u, \chi)$. The
characteristic polynomial is $P(\lambda;\kappa) = \lambda^3 + c_2\lambda^2
+ c_1\lambda + c_0$ with coefficients $c_i(\kappa)$ functions of the
$G$-partials and $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\kappa_v)$. Liu's criterion
(equivalent to Routh--Hurwitz with one zero) gives the Hopf threshold
$\kappa^\star = \{\kappa>0: H(\kappa) := c_1 c_2 - c_0 = 0,\
H'(\kappa)\neq 0,\ c_2(\kappa)>0,\ c_0(\kappa)>0\}$, with imaginary pair
$\lambda_\pm = \pm i\omega^\star$, $\omega^\star = \sqrt{c_1(\kappa^\star)}$.

\begin{theorem}[Hopf bifurcation in the gamma-coupled SV skeleton]
\label{thm:hopf}
Under regularity (A1) $G,\sigma^2 \in C^3$ with $\sigma^2$ bounded below;
(A2) locally unique equilibrium with non-degenerate linearisation;
(A3)~$\kappa\mapsto(a,G_\chi,G_v)$ in $C^3$;
(A4) $\kappa^\star\in(0,\kappa_{\max})$ satisfying the Liu criterion with
$\omega^\star>0$; (A5) $\ell_1(\kappa^\star)\neq 0$:
the equilibrium undergoes a Hopf bifurcation at $\kappa = \kappa^\star$.
On a one-sided neighbourhood of $\kappa^\star$ there is a smooth family of
periodic orbits $\Gamma_\kappa$ of period $T_\kappa = 2\pi/\omega^\star +
O(\kappa - \kappa^\star)$ and amplitude
$\propto \sqrt{|\kappa-\kappa^\star|}$; orbits are stable iff
$\ell_1(\kappa^\star)<0$ (supercritical), supplying endogenous volatility
cycles in $u_t = v_t-\theta_v$. Proof: implicit function theorem on $P$
plus \citet{kuznetsov2004} normal-form reduction; full version on arXiv.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Closed-form $\kappa^\star$ and $\ell_1$.} For the natural
parametric specification---log-normal open-interest in log-strike with
density $q(\log K)\propto\exp(-(\log K-\mu_q)^2/(2\sigma_q^2))$ at a
representative maturity $T_{\mathrm{eff}}$---the Gaussian-product identity
collapses the OI integral to
$G(a,v) = \mathcal{C}(v)\,e^{-a}\,\exp(-(a-m(v))^2/(2\tau^2(v)))$
with $\tau^2 = \sigma_q^2 + v\,T_{\mathrm{eff}}$. The Routh--Hurwitz
polynomial becomes a quadratic in $\kappa$, giving
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\kappa^\star = \frac{G_y A^2 - G_v L \;-\; \sqrt{(G_v L - G_y A^2)^2 - 4G_y^2 A(MA - L/2)}}{2 G_y^2 A},\;}
\label{eq:lognorm-kappastar}
\end{equation}
with $A=\alpha+\kappa_v$, $M=\alpha\kappa_v$, $L=\beta\gamma$, where the
sub-radical is the first parametric phase-boundary discriminant: $D<0$
$\Rightarrow$ no Hopf at any $\kappa$. Substituting the $G$-partials of
the closed-form aggregator into the Kuznetsov bilinear/trilinear formula
yields a $\sim\!30$-term closed-form rational $\ell_1(\kappa^\star)$ in
$(G_y,G_v,G_{aa},G_{av},G_{vv},G_{aaa},G_{aav},G_{avv},G_{vvv},
\kappa_v,\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$, evaluated in $\sim\!50\,\mu$s per parameter
tuple and matching the FD-tensor pipeline to $<0.6\%$. At the canonical
regime $(\sigma_q{=}0.10, T_{\mathrm{eff}}{=}0.25, \kappa_v{=}2,
\alpha{=}0.05, \beta{=}1, \gamma{=}1, \mu_q{=}\log 100, v^\star{=}0.04)$:
$\kappa^\star = 17.81$, $\omega^\star = 1.18$~rad/yr,
$\ell_1 = -4.81\times 10^{-1}$ (supercritical).

\paragraph{Codim-2 and mean-field nods.} A $71\times 97$ grid scan in
$(\sigma_q,\gamma)\in[0.05,0.40]\times[0.20,5.00]$ closes the codim-2
structure: the Bautin curve $\ell_1=0$ partitions the region with
sub-critical dominating $\sim\!5{:}1$, and saddle-node coupling
$\kappa_{\mathrm{SN}}\leq -1.31$ at every cell, so the
\emph{Bogdanov--Takens locus is empty in the canonical scan window}
(Theorem~2, full version on arXiv). The McKean--Vlasov mean-field limit
($n_{\mathrm{dealers}}\to\infty$, propagation of chaos
\citep[Ch.~1]{sznitman1991}, validated empirically at the theoretical
$n^{-1/2}$ Sznitman rate) extends the Jacobian to 4D; the
\citet{liu1994} 4D Hopf condition gives a regime-dependent threshold
ratio $\rho(\theta_G) := \kappa^\star_{\mathrm{MV}}/\kappa^\star_{\mathrm{single}}$
with $\rho(\theta_G)<1$ strictly at the canonical short-gamma regime
($G_y>0$, every finite $\theta_G>0$), recovering
$\rho\to 1$ at instantaneous hedging. The single-dealer model therefore
\emph{under-predicts} instability at the benchmark SPX setting ---
reversing the v0.3.5 claim that mean-field always raises the threshold
(Theorem~3, full statement and derivation on arXiv).

\paragraph{Hawkes criticality and the SV bifurcation endpoint.} The
reflexive feedback has a self-exciting (Hawkes) representation, and the
onset of endogeneity is its approach to criticality. Criticality $n\to 1$
is the loss of a stable direction of the SV drift---an eigenvalue of the
drift-Jacobian $J(\kappa)$ reaching the imaginary axis---and the boundary
carries two distinct strata: a non-oscillatory \emph{real-eigenvalue}
(saddle-node) crossing, and a strictly stronger \emph{Hopf} crossing (a
complex pair, $\operatorname{Re}\lambda=0$, $\operatorname{Im}\lambda\neq0$).

\begin{theorem}[Hawkes--SV criticality correspondence]
\label{thm:hawkes-sv}
Under the diffusive near-critical limit of
\citet{bacrydelattrehoffmannmuzy2013,jaissonrosenbaum2015}, Hawkes
branching-ratio criticality $n\to 1$ coincides with the real-eigenvalue
(saddle-node) stratum of the SV drift boundary---the literal analogue of
\citet{hardiman2013}. The Hopf stratum is a strictly stronger,
oscillatory instability beyond any scalar branching ratio: it has no
single-real-number representation and is the genuinely new
``unoccupied cell'' of the endogeneity--bifurcation correspondence.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Position, not identity.} A scalar branching ratio can only
encode a real crossing, so the oscillatory Hopf endpoint is invisible to
it by construction; we therefore claim a structural position, not a
verified numerical identity (an earlier draft's dimensionless ratio, true
by definition, was a tautology and is removed---amendment~A10). In our
dealer-gamma model the saddle-node locus is itself unphysical at the
canonical specification ($\kappa_{\mathrm{SN}}\leq -1.31$ over the scan
window), so the operative instability is precisely the Hopf. A falsifiable
spectral discriminator (\texttt{classify\_stratum}) separates the Hopf
side from the real/stable side with zero overlap on simulator ground
truth; we claim no realised Hopf edge in any dataset, only the statistic
that would detect one. Whether the market sits near $\kappa^\star$ is
deferred to the empirical dealer-gamma test (amendment~A9).

\section{Numerical phase diagram}
\label{sec:phase}

We sweep $\kappa\in[0,2]$ on a 401-point grid for each of
$31\times 21\times 4 = 2{,}604$ cells in $(\xi,\rho,\sigma_v)$ at the
representative regime $\{G_x,G_v,G_\chi\}=\{0.5,-0.5,-0.5\}$, $\alpha=0.5$,
$\beta=1$, $\gamma=0.5$, $\kappa_v=2$ (constant-vol surrogate). The pair
$(\xi,\rho)$ enters via the leading shear correction $a_{\mathrm{eff}}(\kappa)=
a(\kappa)+\tfrac{1}{2}\xi^2\rho G_v$, an
\citet{baxendale2025}-style projection of small-noise stochastic
Hopf onto the eigenvalue envelope.

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/hopf_phase_diagram.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Hopf threshold $\kappa^\star$ across $(\xi,\rho,\sigma_v)$.}
\emph{Left:} heatmap at $\sigma_v=0.50$. White cells: no Hopf in
$\kappa\in[0,2]$, concentrated in the high-$\xi$ / strongly-negative-$\rho$
wedge where shear destabilises the eigenvalue envelope. \emph{Right:} four

\label{fig:hopf-phase-diagram}

The SPX-relevant $(\xi,\rho)\approx(0.30,-0.70)$ corner has
$\kappa^\star$ in the $0.85$--$0.95$ band. Whether the empirical market
sits near this Hopf boundary is deferred to the empirical dealer-gamma
test; \cref{thm:hawkes-sv} places \citet{hardiman2013}'s $n\approx 1$ at
the distinct real-eigenvalue (saddle-node) edge, in the
endogenous-feedback tradition of
\citet{brockhommes1998,filimonov2012,bouchaud2011endo}. Khasminskii-style
sphere-process estimates of the stochastic-Hopf shift give $|\Lambda|\in
[5\times 10^{-2}, 9\times 10^{-2}]$ on a $6\times 6$ $(\xi,\rho)$ grid;
$|\Lambda|\cdot\varepsilon^2\ll 1$ at calibration-noise scale, so the
deterministic $\kappa^\star$ is the operative threshold.

\section{Pre-registered evaluation framework}
\label{sec:eval}

The pipeline is committed at git commit \texttt{268c061} with an
OpenTimestamps Bitcoin-anchored proof of the document hash, prior to any
contact with empirical SPX data, adapting the \citet{camerer2018}
template to RL-for-derivatives. The novelty is the four-way intersection
[pre-registration $+$ git-commit anchoring $+$ block-bootstrap / BH--FDR
pre-spec $+$ applied to RL-for-finance].

\paragraph{Primitives \& hypotheses.}
(i) Sliced-$W_2$ \citep{bonneel2015sliced} over arbitrage-free 21-day
surface windows---the realism metric. (ii) $\kappa$-sensitivity slope at
calibrated $\kappa_0$---the reflexivity-importance scalar. The closed-form
$\kappa^\star$ elasticity $\eta_{\mu_q}=+703$ makes calibration of the OI
mean to within $\sim 5$~bp of ATM in log-strike the binding observational
requirement for $\pm 5\%$ tolerance on $\kappa^\star$.
\textbf{H1$'$} (primary, direct dealer-gamma regression, amendment~A9): a
model-free regression of next-day realised vol-of-vol on standardised
signed dealer GEX from the OI grid (no RL, no simulator), pooled over the
three event windows with a quiet-regime control; the mechanism predicts a
negative coefficient, required at $p<0.05$ under both a moving-block
bootstrap and Newey--West HAC (synthetic pooled power $0.86$, sign $0.98$,
FPR $0.02$). \textbf{H1} (secondary realism, superseded as primary): a
model-free RL agent $\pi_{\kappa_0}$ trained inside the reflexive simulator
beats four non-reflexive baselines on sliced-$W_2$ over rolling 21-day
windows at Volmageddon, COVID, and the 2024 yen-carry unwind. Decision rule: 12 pairwise dominance checks with
non-overlapping 95\% block-bootstrap CIs \citep{politisromano1994};
arbitrage filter follows \citet{roper2010,gatheraljacquier2014,lee2004};
sample-complexity follows \citet{manole2022sw}.
\textbf{H2}: $\kappa$-sensitivity TOST equivalence on the dimensionless
elasticity with GP-posterior slope CI under the pre-registered RBF +
WhiteKernel kernel (A6) and elliptic uncertainty sets \citep{ma2025robust}.
\textbf{H4} (critical slowing down, amendment~A8): the Hopf limit-cycle
period ($5.3$--$11$\,yr) lies below any usable window's resolution, so the
spectral peak is replaced by the rolling lag-1 autocorrelation of $|r_t|$
with a Kendall-$\tau$ trend vs an AR(1)/phase-randomised surrogate null
\citep{scheffer2009ews,dakos2012methods}; FPR $\leq 0.083$ and
$0.82$--$0.85$ power on a $252$-day record, exploratory within the bare
event window.
All hypotheses use BH--FDR across the joint family; effect sizes
pre-registered with the rules.

\paragraph{Synthetic-pipeline validation.} Before empirical contact, we
exercise the locked pipeline on three synthetic sources:
(a) $\kappa_0$-trained agent re-deployed at $\kappa_0$ (reference);
(b) same agent at $4\kappa_0$ (mild reflexive shift);
(c) Heston (different mechanism). On the production budget (BC: 30
episodes; per source: 30 rollouts $\times$ 100 days; 200 stationary
block-bootstrap resamples), the locked rule returns the expected
ordering with non-overlapping CIs:
$\mathrm{SW2}_a = 0.00498$ (CI $[0.00431, 0.0164]$),
$\mathrm{SW2}_b = 0.0337$ (CI $[0.0300, 0.0402]$),
$\mathrm{SW2}_c = 0.0541$ (CI $[0.0434, 0.0611]$).
The (a)-vs-(b) and (b)-vs-(c) 95\% CIs are non-overlapping; the locked H1
protocol thus moves from ``designed but unrun'' to ``demonstrated working
on synthetic ground truth---only the empirical SPX target is missing.''

\section{Mechanism decomposition and conclusions}
\label{sec:mechanism}

\citet{halperin2025marketron} is a different mechanism: their memory
carrier $y$ enters both drifts non-linearly through a potential $V_M(x)$,
while our $\chi$ enters only the variance drift via $\gamma \chi$, and our
$\kappa=\gamma=0$ collapse is standard Heston while Marketron's is not.
A 5D $(\kappa,\gamma,T_{\mathrm{eff}},\mu_q,\sigma_q)$ mechanism
decomposition gives an all-cell, out-of-sample shape-feature sign-agreement
rate of $7/28$ (worse than chance --- we do not claim otherwise; the
all-cell aggregate is the wrong aggregator). The a priori restricted
subset (long-horizon $T\geq 0.5$~yr, shape-only, within-envelope,
dead-zone-symmetric) gives $4/8$ OOS ($p\approx 0.637$, chance-level)
and $6/8$ in-sample. The predictable long-horizon-skew sign disagreement
under risk-neutral drift (Bessembinder compounding absent) is the
falsifiable Phase~4 prediction. This supersedes the earlier inflated
one-third headline.

We have introduced a 3D reflexive SV simulator, proved a Hopf bifurcation
theorem with closed-form $\ell_1$ for log-normal OI, closed the codim-2
Bautin/BT structure (BT-empty in the canonical window) and the corrected
McKean--Vlasov threshold (destabilising at the canonical short-gamma
regime, stabilising at long-gamma log-normal-OI),
and placed the empirical \citet{hardiman2013} branching ratio at the
real-eigenvalue (saddle-node) stratum, with the model's Hopf the strictly
stronger oscillatory cell (\cref{thm:hawkes-sv}). The evaluation
pipeline---a direct dealer-gamma regression as primary test, with
sliced-$W_2$ and a critical-slowing-down detector---is pre-registered with
an OpenTimestamps proof; synthetic-ground-truth validation of the locked
surface-distance (secondary) protocol returns the predicted ordering. Phase-4 SPX results pre-committed
to publication regardless of direction. Proofs, closed-form $\ell_1$, and
code at [anonymous repository link].
