Title: Gamma-Shaped Dealer-Book Pressure and Endogenous Volatility Cycles: A Reduced-Form Fixed-Equilibrium Hopf Model and a Pre-Extraction Predictive Protocol

Abstract (plain ASCII text for the arXiv form; 1,792 characters):

We study a reduced-form three-state mechanism in which gamma-shaped option-book pressure interacts with a filtered price--variance loop and can generate an endogenous cycle near a fixed reference state. Under the physical measure, the states are detrended log price, instantaneous variance with square-root diffusion, and an exponentially filtered price signal. Centering the book-pressure functional leaves the equilibrium unchanged as coupling varies, while scaling memory feedback by variance preserves inward drift at zero variance. For any three-times continuously differentiable book functional, we derive the cubic Routh--Hurwitz conditions for a Hopf bifurcation, the exact eigenvalue crossing speed, and the first Lyapunov coefficient governing the stability of emerging cycles. A Gaussian density of latent signed positions yields a quadratic threshold equation. In a deliberately noncalibrated example, direct integration of the nonlinear drift produces an attracting cycle whose local amplitude follows the predicted square-root scaling. Signed-mixture checks show strong dependence on book shape and orientation: some mixtures are subcritical, and reversing the canonical orientation removes positive local Hopf roots. The option kernel shapes a postulated pressure term; it does not derive hedge flow or market impact, and the price-to-variance channel is a separate modeling assumption. The numerical result therefore establishes internal feasibility for the specified loop rather than an S&P 500 calibration. A separate preregistered protocol uses public open interest to study participant-sign-free predictive associations before proprietary-data extraction. Dealer inventory and causal effects lie outside its estimand, and the registered market data have not been accessed.
