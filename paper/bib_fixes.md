## P0-2 (3P-MATH): Theorem 4 / §3.11 BDHM citation needs reframing

The relevant `main.tex` site is **line 370**, currently reading:

> `... converges to an Ornstein--Uhlenbeck SDE \citep[Theorem 2]{bacrydelattrehoffmannmuzy2013}: $d\bar\lambda = -\beta(1-n)\bar\lambda\,dt + \sigma_\lambda\, dW$.`

Per the 3P-MATH audit, BDHM 2013 Theorem 2 is a *stationary FCLT*, not the near-critical OU limit needed here. The actual OU form *is* a direct calculation from BDHM 2013 Section 2 (the exponential-kernel intensity is itself the solution of a linear SDE — the OU form is mechanical from the kernel choice, not the stationary CLT). The canonical multivariate near-critical result is Jaisson–Rosenbaum 2015 (which gives integrated CIR in the limit, not OU — the OU specialisation is the 1D exponential-kernel case).

### Recommended rewrite (main.tex line 370)

```latex
% BEFORE
... converges to an Ornstein--Uhlenbeck SDE \citep[Theorem 2]{bacrydelattrehoffmannmuzy2013}: $d\bar\lambda = -\beta(1-n)\bar\lambda\,dt + \sigma_\lambda\, dW$.

% AFTER  (suggested wording — main agent should tune)
... satisfies a linear SDE that, on the rescaled time/intensity, takes the Ornstein--Uhlenbeck form
$d\bar\lambda = -\beta(1-n)\bar\lambda\,dt + \sigma_\lambda\, dW$.
This is immediate from the exponential-kernel intensity dynamics analysed in
\citet[\S2]{bacrydelattrehoffmannmuzy2013}; the near-critical scaling $n \to 1$
under which the deterministic relaxation rate $\beta(1 - n)$ vanishes is the
1-D exponential-kernel specialisation of the integrated-CIR limit of
\citet{jaissonrosenbaum2015}.
```

The main agent should pick the closest BDHM 2013 §2 proposition/equation number when proofreading against the journal PDF — I was unable to fetch the BDHM 2013 full text through the redirect chain.
