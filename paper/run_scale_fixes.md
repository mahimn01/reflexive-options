## Fix 1 — McKean–Vlasov propagation-of-chaos run scale (P0)

### Problem

`paper/main.tex` §3.10 claimed an `n`-particle Euler simulation across
`n ∈ {10, 32, 100, 316, 1000}` at 64 replicates per `n` (the production-mode
config of `MVValidationConfig`). The actual runs in
`runs/mckean_vlasov_validation/*` and the pinned numbers in
`tests/repro/baseline_v0.3.3.json` were generated by `quick=True`
(`n_grid=(10, 100, 1000)`, `n_replicates=16`, `n_steps=100`) — a 3-point fit
with 4× fewer replicates than the paper described. The cited fitted slope
`-0.58` came from that quick fit; the paper's headline statistical-power claim
was overstated.

### Action taken

Re-ran the production sweep:

```
uv run python -m reflexive_options.experiments.mckean_vlasov_validation
```

at the dataclass defaults `n_grid=(10, 32, 100, 316, 1000)`,
`n_replicates=64`, `n_steps=250`. Walltime ≈ 2 s on the dev laptop (the
audit's 10–30 min budget estimate was conservative by 3 orders of magnitude —
the inner loop is vectorised in numpy).

Outputs:

- `runs/mckean_vlasov_validation/20260526T122905Z/{config,metrics,scaling}.{json,npz}`
- `paper/figures/mckean_vlasov_propagation_chaos.pdf` (regenerated from the
  production run)
- `tests/repro/baseline_v0.3.3.json` MV section updated to pin the 5-point
  production-scale numbers (with new `rmse_sup_n32` and `rmse_sup_n316`
  metrics added to the receipt).
- `src/reflexive_options/experiments/_generate_repro_baseline_v033.py`
  switched from `_run(quick=True)` to `_run(quick=False)` and metric
  extractors updated for the 5-point grid.

## Fix 3 — GP-CI σ=0.05 multi-seed claim (P1 + P0-7)

### Problem

§5 H2 paragraph (line 518) claimed:

> the nominal-95\% RBF-GP slope CI [is] at empirical 91.5--100\% coverage on
> smooth-truth functions ($\sigma=0.10$ noise, single-seed condition) but
> drops to 67--94\% under tighter $\sigma=0.05$ multi-seed conditions

The σ=0.10 single-seed numbers exist in
`runs/gp_ci_coverage/20260514T193000Z/coverage_table.json`. The σ=0.05
multi-seed numbers had **no run on disk**. Claim was unsourced.

### Action taken — chose path (A): re-run

Extended `scripts/gp_ci_coverage_audit.py` with `--sigma`, `--n-seeds`,
`--n-reps`, and `--seed` CLI flags. The audit now averages per-truth /
per-method coverage across `n_seeds` independent top-level seeds and reports
both the mean and (min, max) range. Output now includes `per_seed` for
downstream re-aggregation.

Ran the production σ=0.05 multi-seed sweep:

```
uv run python scripts/gp_ci_coverage_audit.py --sigma 0.05 --n-seeds 30 --n-reps 200
```

Output: `runs/gp_ci_coverage/20260526T123226Z_sigma0.05_seeds30/`.

### New GP-CI σ=0.05 mean-coverage table (across 30 seeds, 200 reps each)

| truth     | gp_pinned_rbf | gp_inflated | bca_bootstrap | gp_matern32 |
|---        |---            |---          |---            |---          |
| quadratic | 0.900         | 0.900       | 0.950         | 1.000       |
| linear    | 0.899         | 0.899       | 0.944         | 1.000       |
| sin       | 0.958         | 0.958       | 0.000         | 1.000       |
| quintic   | 0.956         | 0.956       | 0.064         | 1.000       |
| kinked    | 1.000         | 1.000       | 1.000         | 1.000       |

Per-seed (min, max) range for the RBF-GP method on the smooth basket
(quadratic, linear, sin, quintic):

- quadratic: 0.840 / 0.930
- linear:    0.855 / 0.935
- sin:       0.905 / 0.985
- quintic:   0.925 / 0.980

`κ_inf` calibration constant: 1.000 (so `gp_inflated` is identical to
`gp_pinned_rbf` at this regime — the smooth basket does not need inflation).

### Verdict

The paper's claim of **67–94% coverage at σ=0.05 multi-seed** is **wrong**.
The actual mean-coverage range on the smooth basket is **89.9–95.8%** (with
the kinked truth at 100%). The per-seed minimum on the smooth basket is
84% (quadratic), and 30 of 30 seeds × 5 truths land in [0.84, 1.00] on the
RBF-GP method — strictly better than the false "67–94%" range.

This means the H2 paragraph's pessimistic framing should be **softened**.
Coverage at σ=0.05 mildly under-covers nominal-95% on the easy truths
(linear, quadratic both at ~90%) and over-covers slightly on harder smooth
truths (sin, quintic at ~96%) — typical finite-sample GP-CI behaviour and
within the band a Phase-4 H2 audit could absorb.

The BCa bootstrap remains catastrophic on oscillatory truths (0% on `sin`,
6% on `quintic`) — confirming the v0.3.2 design call to keep the RBF kernel
as the locked H2 method.

### Required edits to `paper/main.tex`

**Line 518** (single sentence inside the H2 limitation note). Replace

> ...show the nominal-95\% RBF-GP slope CI at empirical 91.5--100\% coverage
> on smooth-truth functions ($\sigma=0.10$ noise, single-seed condition) but
> drops to 67--94\% under tighter $\sigma=0.05$ multi-seed conditions;

with

> ...show the nominal-95\% RBF-GP slope CI at empirical 91.5--100\% coverage
> on smooth-truth functions ($\sigma=0.10$ noise, single-seed condition), and
> remains at 89.9--100\% mean coverage on the same basket under the tighter
> $\sigma=0.05$ regime aggregated across 30 independent seeds (per-seed range
> 84--98.5\% on the smooth-truth subset; \texttt{runs/gp\_ci\_coverage/}*\texttt{\_sigma0.05\_seeds30/});

No other text in the H2 paragraph needs changing. The rest of the
limitation note (Matérn-3/2 vacuous-CI behaviour, A1–A7 lock) is unchanged.
